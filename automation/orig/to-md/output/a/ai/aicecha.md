Risolviamo l'equazione
$\textcolor{red}{z^2 - 17z + 16 = 0}$
applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = -17}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = 16}$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-(-17) \pm \sqrt{(-17)^2 - 4(1)(16)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{17 \pm \sqrt{289 - 64}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{17 \pm \sqrt{225}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{17 \pm 15}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il più ed una volta il meno

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_1 = \frac{17 + 15}{2} = \frac{32}{2} = 16}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_2 = \frac{17 - 15}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
$\textcolor{red}{z_1 = 3 \quad z_2 = 4}$
