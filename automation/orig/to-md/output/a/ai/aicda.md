Risolviamo l'equazione

$\textcolor{red}{x^4 - 37x^2 + 36 = 0}$

pongo $\textcolor{red}{x^2 = z}$ e quindi $\textcolor{red}{x^4 = z^2}$ ed ottengo

$\textcolor{red}{z^2 - 37z + 36 = 0}$

risolvo rispetto a $z$

$$
\textcolor{red}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{z_{1,2} = \frac{37 \pm \sqrt{37^2 - 4 \cdot 36}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{z_{1,2} = \frac{37 \pm \sqrt{1369 - 144}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{z_{1,2} = \frac{37 \pm \sqrt{1225}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{z_{1,2} = \frac{37 \pm 35}{2}}
$$

- $\textcolor{red}{z_1 = \frac{37 + 35}{2} = \frac{72}{2} = 36}$
- $\textcolor{red}{z_2 = \frac{37 - 35}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1}$

Ora, essendo $z = x^2$ devo risolvere le due equazioni:

- $\textcolor{red}{x^2 = 36}$
- $\textcolor{red}{x^2 = 1}$

- risolvo la prima
  $\textcolor{red}{x_1 = -6}$
  $\textcolor{red}{x_2 = +6}$
- risolvo la seconda
  $\textcolor{red}{x_3 = -1}$
  $\textcolor{red}{x_4 = +1}$

quindi le 4 soluzioni (messe in fila) sono:

$\textcolor{red}{x_1 = -6} \quad \textcolor{red}{x_2 = -1} \quad \textcolor{red}{x_3 = 1} \quad \textcolor{red}{x_4 = 6}$
