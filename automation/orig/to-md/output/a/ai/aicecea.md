Risolviamo l'equazione
[$$
\textcolor{red}{t^2 - 2t - 15 = 0}
$$]{.text-red}
applichiamo la formula ridotta

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-(b/2) \pm \sqrt{(b/2)^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$]{.text-blue}

Abbiamo:
[$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{b = -2 \quad b/2 = -1}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{c = -15}$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-(-1) \pm \sqrt{(-1)^2 - (1)(-15)}}{1}}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = 1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 15}}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = 1 \pm \sqrt{16}}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = 1 \pm 4}
$$]{.text-blue}

Ora prendo una volta il meno e una volta il più

[$$\textcolor{blue}{t_1 = 1 - 4 = -3}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{t_2 = 1 + 4 = +5}$$]{.text-blue}

Ho quindi le soluzioni
[$$
\textcolor{red}{t_1 = -3 \quad t_2 = 5}
$$]{.text-red}
