Risolviamo l'equazione
$$
\textcolor{red}{z^2 - 7z + 10 = 0}
$$
applichiamo la formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
[$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{b = -7}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{c = 10}$$]{.text-blue}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-(-7) \pm \sqrt{(-7)^2 - 4(1)(10)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 40}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{9}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm 3}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno e una volta il più
$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_1 = \frac{7 - 3}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_2 = \frac{7 + 3}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
[$$\textcolor{red}{z_1 = 2 \quad z_2 = 5}$$]{.text-red}
