# [PASSAGGI PER CALCOLARE IL CUBO DEL BINOMIO]{.text-red}

$\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = (a+b) \cdot (a+b) \cdot (a+b) =}$

moltiplico fra loro i primi due

$$
\textcolor{red}{a \cdot a = a^2}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{a \cdot b = ab}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{b \cdot a = ab}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{b \cdot b = b^2}
$$

e poiché $\textcolor{red}{ab + ab = 2ab}$

otterrò

$$
\textcolor{red}{= (a^2 + 2ab + b^2) \cdot (a + b) =}
$$

Eseguo la moltiplicazione

$$
\textcolor{red}{a^2 \cdot a = a^3}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{a^2 \cdot b = a^2b}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{2ab \cdot a = 2a^2b}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{2ab \cdot b = 2ab^2}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{b^2 \cdot a = ab^2}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{b^2 \cdot b = b^3}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{= a^3 + a^2b + 2a^2b + 2ab^2 + ab^2 + b^3 =}
$$

ora sommo i termini simili

$$
\textcolor{red}{a^2b + 2a^2b = 3a^2b}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{2ab^2 + ab^2 = 3ab^2}
$$

quindi

$$
\textcolor{red}{= a^3 + 3a^2b + 3ab^2 + b^3}
$$
