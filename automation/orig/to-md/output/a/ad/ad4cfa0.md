# [CALCOLIAMO]{.text-red}

---

I numeri sotto sono la somma dei $2$ numeri sopra oppure, se sopra c'è un solo numero valgono $1$
infatti:

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 1+1 \quad 1}
$$

per la riga successiva

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 1+2 \quad 2+1 \quad 1}
$$

e così via di seguito

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 3 \quad 3 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^4 = 1 \quad 1+3 \quad 3+3 \quad 3+1 \quad 1}
$$
