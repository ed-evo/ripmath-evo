risolvo la prima equazione
$$
\textcolor{blue}{3x^2 + 4x\sqrt{3} - 12 = 0}
$$
applico la formula ridotta
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-\beta \pm \sqrt{\beta^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$
abbiamo:
$\textcolor{blue}{a = 3}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = -4\sqrt{3}} \quad \textcolor{blue}{\beta = -2\sqrt{3}}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = -12}$
sostituiamo nella formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-(-2\sqrt{3}) \pm \sqrt{(-2\sqrt{3})^2 - 3(-12)}}{3}}
$$
eseguo i calcoli
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{2\sqrt{3} \pm \sqrt{12+36}}{3}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{2\sqrt{3} \pm \sqrt{48}}{3}}
$$
[scompongo in fattori](afccggc2a.html) e poi [estraggo di radice](../ak/akdf.html)
$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{2\sqrt{3} \pm 4\sqrt{3}}{3}}
$$
adesso devo prendere una volta il meno e una volta il più
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 4\sqrt{3}}{3} = -\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{2\sqrt{3} + 4\sqrt{3}}{3} = 2\sqrt{3}}
$$
