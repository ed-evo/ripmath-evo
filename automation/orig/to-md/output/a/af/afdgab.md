Risolviamo: [$2x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$]{.text-blue}

Applico la formula risolutiva

$$
\textcolor{red}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

[$a = 2 \quad b = -5 \quad c = 2$]{.text-blue}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot 2}}{2 \cdot 2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 16}}{4}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{9}}{4}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm 3}{4}}
$$

- [$x_1 = \frac{5-3}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$]{.text-blue}
- [$x_2 = \frac{5+3}{4} = \frac{8}{4} = 2$]{.text-blue}
