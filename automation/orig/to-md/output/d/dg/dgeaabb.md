risolviamo l'equazione

$$
\textcolor{red}{m^2 + 20m + 36 = 0}
$$

applichiamo la formula ridotta

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-(b/2) \pm \sqrt{(b/2)^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$

Abbiamo:

$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = -20} \quad \textcolor{blue}{b/2 = -10}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = 36}$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{m_{1,2} = \frac{-(-10) \pm \sqrt{(-10)^2 - (1)(36)}}{1}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{m_{1,2} = 10 \pm \sqrt{100 - 36}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{m_{1,2} = 10 \pm \sqrt{64}}
$$

$\textcolor{blue}{m_{1,2} = 10 \pm 8}$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più ed ottengo

$$
\textcolor{red}{m_1 = 10 - 8 = 2} \quad \textcolor{red}{m_2 = 10 + 8 = 18}
$$
