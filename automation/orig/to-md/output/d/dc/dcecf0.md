$$
\textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{2}{3}x + \frac{5}{3}}
$$

- Diamo alla $$x$$ il valore $$2$$
  $$
  \textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{2}{3} \cdot 2 + \frac{5}{3}}
  $$
  $$
  \textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{4}{3} + \frac{5}{3}}
  $$
  $$
  \textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{9}{3} = 3}
  $$
  individuo il punto $$\textcolor{blue}{(2,3)}$$
- Diamo alla $$x$$ il valore $$-4$$
  $$
  \textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{2}{3} \cdot (-4) + \frac{5}{3}}
  $$
  $$
  \textcolor{blue}{y = -\frac{8}{3} + \frac{5}{3}}
  $$
  $$
  \textcolor{blue}{y = -\frac{3}{3} = -1}
  $$
  individuo il punto $$\textcolor{blue}{(-4,-1)}$$
