calcoli:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{5}{\sin 30^\circ} = \frac{b}{\sin 45^\circ}}
$$

faccio il minimo comune multiplo $$\sin 30^\circ \sin 45^\circ$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{5 \sin 45^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ \sin 45^\circ} = \frac{b \sin 30^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ \sin 45^\circ}}
$$

leggo alla rovescia

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{b \sin 30^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ \sin 45^\circ} = \frac{5 \sin 45^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ \sin 45^\circ}}
$$

elimino i denominatori
$$\textcolor{blue}{b \sin 30^\circ = 5 \sin 45^\circ}$$

ricavo $$b$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = \frac{5 \sin 45^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ}}
$$
