# [Tabella di riepilogo sulle formule di Werner]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{red}{\sin \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{\sin(\alpha + \beta) + \sin(\alpha - \beta)}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{\sin(\alpha + \beta) - \sin(\alpha - \beta)}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\cos \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{\cos(\alpha + \beta) + \cos(\alpha - \beta)}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\sin \alpha \sin \beta = -\frac{\cos(\alpha + \beta) - \cos(\alpha - \beta)}{2}}
$$
