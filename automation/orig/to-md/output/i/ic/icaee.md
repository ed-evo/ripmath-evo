## [Formula di prostaferesi]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\sin p + \sin q = 2 \sin \frac{p+q}{2} \cos \frac{p-q}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\sin p - \sin q = 2 \cos \frac{p+q}{2} \sin \frac{p-q}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\cos p + \cos q = 2 \cos \frac{p+q}{2} \cos \frac{p-q}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\cos p - \cos q = -2 \sin \frac{p+q}{2} \sin \frac{p-q}{2}}
$$
