applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
[$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{b = 3}$$]{.text-blue}
[$$\textcolor{blue}{c = -4}$$]{.text-blue}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{(-3)^2 - 4(1)(-4)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9+16}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{25}}{2}}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm 5}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{-3 - 5}{2} = \frac{-8}{2} = -4}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{-3 + 5}{2} = \frac{+2}{2} = 1}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
[$$\textcolor{red}{x_1 = -4}$$]{.text-red} [$$\textcolor{red}{x_2 = 1}$$]{.text-red}
