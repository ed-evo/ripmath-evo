## Equazione della retta passante per i due punti $$A(0,4)$$ e $$B(-4,1)$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{y - y_1}{y_2 - y_1} = \frac{x - x_1}{x_2 - x_1}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{y - 4}{1 - 4} = \frac{x - 0}{-4 - 0}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{y - 4}{-3} = \frac{x}{-4}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{-4y + 16 = -3x}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{-4y = -3x - 16}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{4y = 3x + 16}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{3}{4}x + 4}
