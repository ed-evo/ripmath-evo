[Trovare la distanza fra i punti $$A(-2,-1)$$ e $$B(2,2)$${.text-blue}]

Scrivo la formula:

$$
\textcolor{red}{\overline{AB} = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}}
$$

So che
[$$A = (-2,-1) = (x_1, y_1)$${.text-blue}]
e che
[$$B = (2,2) = (x_2, y_2)$${.text-blue}]

quindi:
[$$x_1 = -2$${.text-blue}]
[$$y_1 = -1$${.text-blue}]
[$$x_2 = 2$${.text-blue}]
[$$y_2 = 2$${.text-blue}]

sostituisco nella formula:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\overline{AB} = \sqrt{[2 - (-2)]^2 + [2 - (-1)]^2} =}

$$

$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \sqrt{16 + 9} = \sqrt{25}} \textcolor{red}{= 5}
$$
