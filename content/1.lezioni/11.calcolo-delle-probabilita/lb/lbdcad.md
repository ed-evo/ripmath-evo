Calcoliamo

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{3}}
$$

Applico la relazione

$$
\textcolor{green}{\binom{n}{k}} = \textcolor{blue}{\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{3} = \frac{10!}{3!(10-3)!} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7!}{3 \cdot 2 \cdot 1 \cdot 7!} = \frac{10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8}{3 \cdot 2} = 120}
$$
