# Calcoli

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x^2 + 2x - 3 = 0}
$$

Usando la formula completa:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{(-2)^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot (-3)}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4 + 12}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{16}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-2 \pm 4}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = -3}
$$

---

oppure usando la formula ridotta:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x^2 + 2x - 3 = 0}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{(-1)^2 - 1 \cdot (-3)}}{1}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = -1 \pm \sqrt{1+3}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = -1 \pm \sqrt{4}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = -1 \pm 2}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = -3}
$$
