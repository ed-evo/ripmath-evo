Risolviamo l'equazione
$$
\textcolor{red}{x^2 + 11x - 80 = 0}
$$
applichiamo la formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = 11}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = -80}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-11 \pm \sqrt{11^2 - 4(1)(-80)}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-11 \pm \sqrt{121 + 320}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-11 \pm \sqrt{441}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-11 \pm 21}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{-11 - 21}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = -16}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{-11 + 21}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = 5}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
$$
\textcolor{red}{x_1 = -16 \quad x_2 = +5}
$$
