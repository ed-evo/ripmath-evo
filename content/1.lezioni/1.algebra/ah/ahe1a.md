Risolviamo l'equazione

$$
\textcolor{red}{s^2 + 3s - 40 = 0}
$$

applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:

[$$a = 1$$]{.text-blue}
[$$b = 3$$]{.text-blue}
[$$c = -40$$]{.text-blue}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{(3)^2 - 4(1)(-40)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9 + 160}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{169}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm 13}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_1 = \frac{-3 - 13}{2} = \frac{-16}{2} = -8}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_2 = \frac{-3 + 13}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni

[$$s_1 = -8$$]{.text-red} [$$s_2 = 5$$]{.text-red}
