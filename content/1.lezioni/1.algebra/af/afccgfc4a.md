# [esercizi]{.text-red}

Risolviamo l'equazione
$$\textcolor{red}{5k^2 + 20k + 16 = 0}$$
applichiamo la formula risolutiva (ridotta) dell'equazione di secondo grado

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-\beta \pm \sqrt{\beta^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 5}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{\beta = \frac{b}{2} = 10}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = 16}
$$

sostituisco

$$
\textcolor{blue}{k_{1,2} = \frac{-10 \pm \sqrt{(10)^2 - (5)(16)}}{5}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{k_{1,2} = \frac{-10 \pm \sqrt{100 - 80}}{5}}
$$

ottengo

$$
\textcolor{blue}{k = \frac{-10 \pm \sqrt{20}}{5}}
$$

siccome $$20 = 2^2 \cdot 5$$ estraggo di radice il $$2$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{k = \frac{-10 \pm 2\sqrt{5}}{5}}
$$
