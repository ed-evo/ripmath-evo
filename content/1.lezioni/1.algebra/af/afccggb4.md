Risolvo la prima equazione
$$\textcolor{blue}{8x^2 - 6x + 1 = 0}$$

Applico la formula ridotta
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-\beta \pm \sqrt{\beta^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 8}$$, $$\textcolor{blue}{b = -6}$$, $$\textcolor{blue}{\beta = -3}$$, $$\textcolor{blue}{c = 1}$$

Sostituiamo nella formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-(-3) \pm \sqrt{(-3)^2 - 8(1)}}{8}}
$$

Eseguo i calcoli
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{9-8}}{8}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{1}}{8}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{3 \pm 1}{8}}
$$

Adesso devo prendere una volta il più e una volta il meno
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{3 + 1}{8} = \frac{1}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{3 - 1}{8} = \frac{1}{4}}
$$
