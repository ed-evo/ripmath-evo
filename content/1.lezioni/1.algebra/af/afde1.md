# esercizio

Trovare le soluzioni reali delle equazioni
$$
\textcolor{red}{x^3 = -1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{x^3 = -8}
$$

---

Risolvo la prima:
$$
\textcolor{red}{x^3 = -1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{x = \sqrt[3]{-1}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{x = -1}
$$

---

Risolvo la seconda:
$$
\textcolor{red}{x^3 = -8}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{x = \sqrt[3]{-8}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{x = -2}
$$

---

Si hanno due soluzioni reali
$$
\textcolor{red}{x_1 = -2} \quad \text{e} \quad \textcolor{red}{x_2 = -1}
$$
