Risolviamo l'equazione $$\textcolor{red}{y^2 + y - 2 = 0}$$
applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{b = 1}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{c = -2}$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1^2 - 4(1)(-2)}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 8}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{9}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm 3}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_1 = \frac{-1 - 3}{2} = -2}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_2 = \frac{-1 + 3}{2} = 1}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
$$\textcolor{red}{y_1 = -2} \quad \textcolor{red}{y_2 = 1}$$
