Risolviamo l'equazione
$$
\textcolor{red}{z^2 - z - 6 = 0}
$$
applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{b = -1}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{c = -6}$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-(-1) \pm \sqrt{(-1)^2 - 4(1)(-6)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 24}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{25}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{1 \pm 5}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il più ed una volta il meno

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_1 = \frac{1 + 5}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_2 = \frac{1 - 5}{2} = \frac{-4}{2} = -2}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
$$
\textcolor{red}{z_1 = 3 \quad z_2 = -2}
$$
