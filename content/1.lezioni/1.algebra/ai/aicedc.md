Risolvere il sistema:

$$
\textcolor{red}{\begin{cases} x - y = -5 \\ xy = -6 \end{cases}}
$$

Stavolta poniamo $$y = -k$$; otteniamo

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\begin{cases} x - (-k) = -5 \\ x(-k) = -6 \end{cases}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\begin{cases} x + k = -5 \\ -kx = -6 \end{cases}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\begin{cases} x + k = -5 \\ kx = 6 \end{cases}}
$$

È un sistema simmetrico elementare; considero l'equazione associata

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z^2 + 5z + 6 = 0}
$$

risolvo ed ottengo [Calcoli](aicedca.html){.text-red}

$$\textcolor{blue}{z_1 = -2} \quad \textcolor{blue}{z_2 = -3}$$

ho quindi le soluzioni

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\begin{cases} x_1 = -2 \\ k_1 = -3 \end{cases}} \quad \textcolor{blue}{\begin{cases} x_2 = -3 \\ k_2 = -2 \end{cases}}
$$

ora essendo

$$\textcolor{blue}{y = -k}$$

sostituisco ed ottengo

$$
\textcolor{red}{\begin{cases} x_1 = -2 \\ y_1 = 3 \end{cases}} \quad \textcolor{red}{\begin{cases} x_2 = -3 \\ y_2 = 2 \end{cases}}
$$
