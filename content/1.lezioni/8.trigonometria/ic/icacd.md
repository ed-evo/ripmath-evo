# [Tabella di riepilogo formule di bisezione]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos \alpha}{2}}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos \alpha}{2}}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \pm \sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha}}}
$$
