Dobbiamo trovare le coordinate del massimo e del minimo

## Massimo

[$$\textcolor{red}{y = x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 =}$$]{.text-red}

Devo sostituire ad $$x$$ la coordinata trovata:

$$
\textcolor{red}{x = \frac{1 - \sqrt{13}}{3}}
$$

Per motivi di rappresentazione sullo schermo faccio il calcolo a parte per $$x^3$$ ed $$x^2$$.

***

- [$$\textcolor{red}{x^3 = \left[ \frac{1 - \sqrt{13}}{3} \right]^3 =}$$]{.text-red}

  Al numeratore devo fare il cubo di un binomio:

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 - 3\sqrt{13} + 3(\sqrt{13})^2 - (\sqrt{13})^3}{27} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 - 3\sqrt{13} + 39 - 13\sqrt{13}}{27} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{40 - 16\sqrt{13}}{27}}
  $$

- [$$\textcolor{red}{x^2 = \left[ \frac{1 - \sqrt{13}}{3} \right]^2 =}$$]{.text-red}

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 - 2\sqrt{13} + (\sqrt{13})^2}{9} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 - 2\sqrt{13} + 13}{9} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{14 - 2\sqrt{13}}{9}}
  $$

***

Ora eseguo il calcolo completo:

[$$\textcolor{red}{y = x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 =}$$]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{40 - 16\sqrt{13}}{27} - \frac{14 - 2\sqrt{13}}{9} - 4 \cdot \frac{1 - \sqrt{13}}{3} + 4 =}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{40 - 16\sqrt{13}}{27} - \frac{14 - 2\sqrt{13}}{9} - \frac{4 - 4\sqrt{13}}{3} + 4 =}
$$

Minimo comune multiplo $$27$$:

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{40 - 16\sqrt{13} - 42 + 6\sqrt{13} - 36 + 36\sqrt{13} + 108}{27} =}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{70 + 26\sqrt{13}}{27}}
$$

**Coordinate del Massimo**

- [$$\textcolor{blue}{x = \frac{1 - \sqrt{13}}{3}}$$]{.text-blue} valore approssimato circa $$-0,8$$
- [$$\textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{70 + 26\sqrt{13}}{27}}$$]{.text-blue} valore approssimato circa $$6,1$$

## Minimo

[$$\textcolor{red}{y = x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 =}$$]{.text-red}

Devo sostituire ad $$x$$ la coordinata trovata:

$$
\textcolor{red}{x = \frac{1 + \sqrt{13}}{3}}
$$

Anche qui, per motivi di rappresentazione sullo schermo, faccio il calcolo a parte per $$x^3$$ ed $$x^2$$.

***

- [$$\textcolor{red}{x^3 = \left[ \frac{1 + \sqrt{13}}{3} \right]^3 =}$$]{.text-red}

  Al numeratore devo fare il cubo di un binomio:

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 + 3\sqrt{13} + 3(\sqrt{13})^2 + (\sqrt{13})^3}{27} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 + 3\sqrt{13} + 39 + 13\sqrt{13}}{27} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{40 + 16\sqrt{13}}{27}}
  $$

- [$$\textcolor{red}{x^2 = \left[ \frac{1 + \sqrt{13}}{3} \right]^2 =}$$]{.text-red}

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 + 2\sqrt{13} + (\sqrt{13})^2}{9} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{1 + 2\sqrt{13} + 13}{9} =}
  $$

  $$
  \textcolor{red}{\frac{14 + 2\sqrt{13}}{9}}
  $$

***

Ora eseguo il calcolo completo:

[$$\textcolor{red}{y = x^3 - x^2 - 4x + 4 =}$$]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{40 + 16\sqrt{13}}{27} - \frac{14 + 2\sqrt{13}}{9} - 4 \cdot \frac{1 + \sqrt{13}}{3} + 4 =}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{40 + 16\sqrt{13}}{27} - \frac{14 + 2\sqrt{13}}{9} - \frac{4 + 4\sqrt{13}}{3} + 4 =}
$$

Minimo comune multiplo $$27$$:

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{40 + 16\sqrt{13} - 42 - 6\sqrt{13} - 36 - 36\sqrt{13} + 108}{27} =}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{\frac{70 - 26\sqrt{13}}{27}}
$$

**Coordinate del minimo**

- [$$\textcolor{blue}{x = \frac{1 + \sqrt{13}}{3}}$$]{.text-blue} valore approssimato circa $$+1,5$$
- [$$\textcolor{blue}{y = \frac{70 - 26\sqrt{13}}{27}}$$]{.text-blue} valore approssimato circa $$-0,9$$
