tan⁡α2=(p−b)(p−c)bcp(p−a)bc\textcolor{blue}{\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{\frac{(p-b)(p-c)}{bc}}{\frac{p(p-a)}{bc}}}} tan2α​=bcp(p−a)​bc(p−b)(p−c)​​​

tan⁡α2=(p−b)(p−c)bc⋅bcp(p−a)\textcolor{blue}{\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{(p-b)(p-c)}{bc} \cdot \frac{bc}{p(p-a)}}} tan2α​=bc(p−b)(p−c)​⋅p(p−a)bc​​
