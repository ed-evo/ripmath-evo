Formula parametrica per il coseno ​

Partiamo dalla formula di duplicazione per il coseno

cos⁡2x=cos⁡2x−sin⁡2x\textcolor{red}{\cos 2x = \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x} cos2x=cos2x−sin2x

poniamo 2x=α\textcolor{red}{2x = \alpha}2x=α e quindi x=(α/2)\textcolor{red}{x = (\alpha/2)}x=(α/2) Otteniamo

cos⁡α=cos⁡2(α/2)−sin⁡2(α/2)\textcolor{red}{\cos \alpha = \cos^2(\alpha/2) - \sin^2(\alpha/2)} cosα=cos2(α/2)−sin2(α/2)

Voglio trasformare il termine dopo l'uguale in una frazione quindi lo divido per 1 cioè per cos⁡2(α/2)+sin⁡2(α/2)\cos^2(\alpha/2) + \sin^2(\alpha/2)cos2(α/2)+sin2(α/2)

cos⁡α=cos⁡2(α/2)−sin⁡2(α/2)cos⁡2(α/2)+sin⁡2(α/2)\textcolor{red}{\cos \alpha = \frac{\cos^2(\alpha/2) - \sin^2(\alpha/2)}{\cos^2(\alpha/2) + \sin^2(\alpha/2)}} cosα=cos2(α/2)+sin2(α/2)cos2(α/2)−sin2(α/2)​

Divido sia al numeratore che al denominatore per cos⁡2(α/2)\cos^2(\alpha/2)cos2(α/2)

cos⁡α=cos⁡2(α/2)cos⁡2(α/2)−sin⁡2(α/2)cos⁡2(α/2)cos⁡2(α/2)cos⁡2(α/2)+sin⁡2(α/2)cos⁡2(α/2)\textcolor{red}{\cos \alpha = \frac{\frac{\cos^2(\alpha/2)}{\cos^2(\alpha/2)} - \frac{\sin^2(\alpha/2)}{\cos^2(\alpha/2)}}{\frac{\cos^2(\alpha/2)}{\cos^2(\alpha/2)} + \frac{\sin^2(\alpha/2)}{\cos^2(\alpha/2)}}} cosα=cos2(α/2)cos2(α/2)​+cos2(α/2)sin2(α/2)​cos2(α/2)cos2(α/2)​−cos2(α/2)sin2(α/2)​​

Ricordando che

sin⁡xcos⁡x=tan⁡x\frac{\sin x}{\cos x} = \tan x cosxsinx​=tanx

otteniamo

cos⁡α=1−tan⁡2(α/2)1+tan⁡2(α/2)\textcolor{red}{\cos \alpha = \frac{1 - \tan^2(\alpha/2)}{1 + \tan^2(\alpha/2)}} cosα=1+tan2(α/2)1−tan2(α/2)​

Poniamo tan⁡(α/2)=t\tan(\alpha/2) = ttan(α/2)=t ed otteniamo la seconda formula parametrica

cos⁡α=1−t21+t2\textcolor{blue}{\cos \alpha = \frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2}} cosα=1+t21−t2​
