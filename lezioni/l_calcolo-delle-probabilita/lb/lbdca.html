Consideriamo la formula di Newton sostituendo ad nnn il valore 101010

(a+b)10=∑k=0,1,…,10(10k)a10−kbk\textcolor{blue}{(a+b)^{10} = \sum_{k=0,1,\dots,10} \binom{10}{k} a^{10-k}b^k} (a+b)10=k=0,1,…,10∑​(k10​)a10−kbk

Per calcolare i vari termini devo sostituire a kkk i valori 0,1,2,3,…,9,100,1,2,3,\dots,9,100,1,2,3,…,9,10 e, sommando i risultati ottengo lo sviluppo del binomio:

  1. Sostituisco 000(100)a10−0b0=a10\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{0} a^{10-0} b^0} = \textcolor{red}{a^{10}}(010​)a10−0b0=a10 Calcolo del coefficiente

  2. Sostituisco 111(101)a10−1b1=10a9b\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{1} a^{10-1} b^1} = \textcolor{red}{10 a^9 b}(110​)a10−1b1=10a9b Calcolo del coefficiente

  3. Sostituisco 222(102)a10−2b2=45a8b2\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{2} a^{10-2} b^2} = \textcolor{red}{45 a^8 b^2}(210​)a10−2b2=45a8b2 Calcolo del coefficiente

  4. Sostituisco 333(103)a10−3b3=120a7b3\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{3} a^{10-3} b^3} = \textcolor{red}{120 a^7 b^3}(310​)a10−3b3=120a7b3 Calcolo del coefficiente

  5. Sostituisco 444(104)a10−4b4=210a6b4\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{4} a^{10-4} b^4} = \textcolor{red}{210 a^6 b^4}(410​)a10−4b4=210a6b4 Calcolo del coefficiente

  6. Sostituisco 555(105)a10−5b5=252a5b5\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{5} a^{10-5} b^5} = \textcolor{red}{252 a^5 b^5}(510​)a10−5b5=252a5b5 Calcolo del coefficiente

  7. Sostituisco 666(106)a10−6b6=210a4b6\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{6} a^{10-6} b^6} = \textcolor{red}{210 a^4 b^6}(610​)a10−6b6=210a4b6

  8. Sostituisco 777(107)a10−7b7=120a3b7\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{7} a^{10-7} b^7} = \textcolor{red}{120 a^3 b^7}(710​)a10−7b7=120a3b7

  9. Sostituisco 888(108)a10−8b8=45a2b8\textcolor{blue}{\binom{10}{8} a^{10-8} b^8} = \textcolor{red}{45 a^2 b^8}(810​)a10−8b8=45a2b8
