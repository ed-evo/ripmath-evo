Considero la serie convergente a sss

s=a1+a2+a3+a4+…s = a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + a_4 + \dots s=a1​+a2​+a3​+a4​+…

s=s1,k1+sk1+1,k2+sk2+1,k3+…s = s_{1, k_1} + s_{k_1+1, k_2} + s_{k_2+1, k_3} + \dots s=s1,k1​​+sk1​+1,k2​​+sk2​+1,k3​​+…
