Calcoli:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{6}{\sin 30^\circ} = \frac{8}{\sin \beta}}
$$

Faccio il minimo comune multiplo $\sin 30^\circ \sin \beta$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{6 \sin \beta}{\sin 30^\circ \sin \beta} = \frac{8 \sin 30^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ \sin \beta}}
$$

Elimino i denominatori

$$
\textcolor{blue}{6 \sin \beta = 8 \sin 30^\circ}
$$

Ricavo $\sin \beta$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\sin \beta = \frac{8 \sin 30^\circ}{6}}
$$
