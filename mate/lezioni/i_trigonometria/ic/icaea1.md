[devo risolvere il sistema]{.text-red}

$$
\begin{cases}
\textcolor{red}{\alpha} + \textcolor{red}{\beta} = p \\
\textcolor{red}{\alpha} - \textcolor{red}{\beta} = q
\end{cases}
$$

[Usiamo il [metodo di addizione](../../a/ai/aibaab.html); prima sommo]{.text-red}

$$
\begin{array}{r}
\begin{cases}
\textcolor{red}{\alpha} + \textcolor{blue}{\beta} = p \\
+ \quad \textcolor{blue}{+} \quad + \\
\textcolor{red}{\alpha} - \textcolor{blue}{\beta} = q
\end{cases} \\
\hline
\textcolor{red}{2\alpha} = p + q
\end{array}
$$

[ed ottengo]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{red}{\alpha} = \frac{p + q}{2}
$$

[Ora sottraggo termine a termine]{.text-red}

$$
\begin{array}{r}
\begin{cases}
\textcolor{blue}{\alpha} + \textcolor{red}{\beta} = p \\
- \quad - \quad - \\
\textcolor{blue}{\alpha} - \textcolor{red}{\beta} = q
\end{cases} \\
\hline
\textcolor{red}{2\beta} = p - q
\end{array}
$$

[ed ottengo]{.text-red}

$$
\textcolor{red}{\beta} = \frac{p - q}{2}
$$
