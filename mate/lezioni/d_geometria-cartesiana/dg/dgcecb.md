risolviamo: [$x^2 + x - 2 = 0$]{.text-blue}

applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
[$a = 1$]{.text-blue}
[$b = 1$]{.text-blue}
[$c = -2$]{.text-blue}

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1^2 - 4(1)(-2)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 8}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{9}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-1 \pm 3}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il più ed una volta il meno

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{-1 + 3}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{-1 - 3}{2} = \frac{-4}{2} = -2}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
[$x_1 = -2$]{.text-red} [$x_2 = 1$]{.text-red}
