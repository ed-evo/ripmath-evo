risolvo la prima equazione

$$
\textcolor{blue}{6x^2 - 2x - 8 = 0}
$$

applico la formula ridotta

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-\beta \pm \sqrt{\beta^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$

abbiamo:
$\textcolor{blue}{a = 6}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = -2}$ $\textcolor{blue}{\beta = -1}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = -8}$

sostituiamo nella formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-(-1) \pm \sqrt{(-1)^2 - 6(-8)}}{6}}
$$

eseguo i calcoli

$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{49}}{6}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{1 \pm 7}{6}}
$$

adesso devo prendere una volta il meno ed una volta il più

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{1 - 7}{6} = -1}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{1 + 7}{6} = \frac{4}{3}}
$$
