Risolviamo: $y^2 + 9y + 8 = 0$

Applico la formula risolutiva

$$
\textcolor{red}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 1 \quad b = 9 \quad c = 8}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-9 \pm \sqrt{81 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 8}}{2 \cdot 1}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-9 \pm \sqrt{81 - 32}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-9 \pm \sqrt{49}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-9 \pm 7}{2}}
$$

- $\textcolor{blue}{y_1 = \frac{-9 + 7}{2} = \frac{-2}{2} = -1}$
- $\textcolor{blue}{y_2 = \frac{-9 - 7}{2} = \frac{-16}{2} = -8}$
