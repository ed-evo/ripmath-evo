# esercizio

razionalizzare:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{9}{4\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5}}}
$$

Devo moltiplicare sopra e sotto per $\textcolor{blue}{4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{5}}$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{9}{4\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{5}} \cdot \frac{4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{5}}{4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{5}}}
$$

ottengo

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{9(4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{5})}{4^2 \cdot 3 - 2^2 \cdot 5}}
$$

semplifico le radici con i quadrati

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{9(4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{5})}{16 \cdot 3 - 4 \cdot 5}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{9(4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{5})}{48 - 20}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{36\sqrt{3} + 18\sqrt{5}}{28}}
$$

Raccolgo due al numeratore

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{2(18\sqrt{3} + 9\sqrt{5})}{28}}
$$

Semplifico ora per 2 ed ottengo il risultato finale

$$
\textcolor{blue}{\frac{18\sqrt{3} + 9\sqrt{5}}{14}}
$$
