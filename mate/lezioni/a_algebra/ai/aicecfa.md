Risolviamo l'equazione

$$
\textcolor{red}{t^2 + 3t - 70 = 0}
$$

applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = 3}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = -70}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{3^2 - 4(1)(-70)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{9 + 280}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm \sqrt{289}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-3 \pm 17}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_1 = \frac{-3 - 17}{2} = \frac{-20}{2} = -10}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_2 = \frac{-3 + 17}{2} = \frac{14}{2} = 7}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni

$$
\textcolor{red}{t_1 = -10 \quad t_2 = 7}
$$
