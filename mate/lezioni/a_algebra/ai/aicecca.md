Risolviamo l'equazione

$$
\textcolor{red}{z^2 - z - 20 = 0}
$$

applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{s_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = -1}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = -20}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-(-1) \pm \sqrt{(-1)^2 - 4(1)(-20)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{1 + 80}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{81}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{1 \pm 9}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il più ed una volta il meno

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_1 = \frac{1 + 9}{2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_2 = \frac{1 - 9}{2} = \frac{-8}{2} = -4}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni

$$
\textcolor{red}{z_1 = 5 \quad z_2 = -4}
$$
