# CALCOLO DEI TERMINI DEL TRIANGOLO DI TARTAGLIA

Considerando le potenze che hai già calcolato, hai i seguenti valori:

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 3 \quad 3 \quad 1}
$$

Per calcolare i termini successivi basta fare:

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 3 \quad 3 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^4 = 1 \quad 1+3 \quad 3+3 \quad 3+1 \quad 1}
$$

cioè

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 3 \quad 3 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^4 = 1 \quad 4 \quad 6 \quad 4 \quad 1}
$$

Proseguendo avrò:

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 3 \quad 3 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^4 = 1 \quad 4 \quad 6 \quad 4 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^5 = 1 \quad 1+4 \quad 4+6 \quad 6+4 \quad 4+1 \quad 1}
$$

cioè

$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^0 = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^1 = 1 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^2 = 1 \quad 2 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^3 = 1 \quad 3 \quad 3 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^4 = 1 \quad 4 \quad 6 \quad 4 \quad 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{(a+b)^5 = 1 \quad 5 \quad 10 \quad 10 \quad 5 \quad 1}
$$
