Risolviamo l'equazione

[$$
\textcolor{red}{5x^2 - 72x - 1701 = 0}
$$]{.text-red}

conviene applicare la formula ridotta

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-\frac{b}{2} \pm \sqrt{(\frac{b}{2})^2 - ac}}{a}}
$$]{.text-blue}

Abbiamo:

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 5}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = -72 \quad \frac{b}{2} = -36}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = -1701}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-(-36) \pm \sqrt{(-36)^2 - (5)(-1701)}}{5}}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{36 \pm \sqrt{1296 + 8505}}{5}}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{36 \pm \sqrt{9801}}{5}}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{36 \pm 99}{5}}
$$]{.text-blue}

Ora prendo una volta il più ed una volta il meno

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{36 + 99}{5} = \frac{135}{5} = 27}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{36 - 99}{5} = -\frac{63}{5}}
$$]{.text-blue}

Ho quindi le soluzioni

[$$
\textcolor{red}{x_1 = 27 \quad x_2 = -\frac{63}{5}}
$$]{.text-red}
