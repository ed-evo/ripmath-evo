$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-\beta \pm \sqrt{\beta^2 - ac}}{a}}

$\textcolor{blue}{a = \sqrt[3]{7^2}}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = 2 \sqrt[3]{7} \quad \beta = \sqrt[3]{7}}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = 4}$
