> **Esempio:**
>
> nel triangolo $$ABC$$ ho
> [$$\textcolor{blue}{a = 5 \text{ cm.}}$$ $$\textcolor{blue}{b = 7 \text{ cm.}}$$ $$\textcolor{blue}{\gamma = 60^\circ}$$]{.text-blue}
>
> devo trovare $$c$$, imposto la relazione
>
> $$
> \textcolor{blue}{c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos \gamma}
> $$
>
> $$
> \textcolor{blue}{c^2 = 5^2 + 7^2 - 2(5)(7) \cos 60^\circ}
> $$
>
> $$
> \textcolor{blue}{c^2 = 25 + 49 - 70(1/2)}
> $$
>
> $$
> \textcolor{blue}{c^2 = 25 + 49 - 35}
> $$
>
> $$
> \textcolor{blue}{c^2 = 39}
> $$
>
> estraendo la radice e considerando solo la soluzione positiva (è un segmento!)
>
> $$
> \textcolor{blue}{c = \sqrt{39}}
> $$
