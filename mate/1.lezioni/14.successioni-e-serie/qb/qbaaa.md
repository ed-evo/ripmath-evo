# Calcoli

Primo termine $$-2$$

Sommiamo la ragione $$+\frac{1}{2}$$ al primo termine e ad ogni termine successivo.

Secondo termine
$$
-2 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{-4+1}{2} = -\frac{3}{2}
$$

Terzo termine
$$
-\frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{-3+1}{2} = -\frac{2}{2} = -1
$$

Quarto termine
$$
-1 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{-2+1}{2} = -\frac{1}{2}
$$

Quinto termine
$$
-\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{-1+1}{2} = 0
$$

Sesto termine
$$
0 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

Settimo termine
$$
\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1+1}{2} = \frac{2}{2} = 1
$$

Ottavo termine
$$
1 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2+1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}
$$
