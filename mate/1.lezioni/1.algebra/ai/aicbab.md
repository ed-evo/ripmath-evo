Risolviamo l'equazione $\textcolor{red}{8y^2 + 31y + 23 = 0}$
applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$\textcolor{blue}{a = 8}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = 31}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = 23}$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-31 \pm \sqrt{31^2 - 4(8)(23)}}{16}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-31 \pm \sqrt{961 - 736}}{16}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-31 \pm \sqrt{225}}{16}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{-31 \pm 15}{16}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_1 = \frac{-31 - 15}{16} = -\frac{23}{8}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_2 = \frac{-31 + 15}{16} = -1}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni:
$\textcolor{red}{y_1 = -23/8}$ $\textcolor{red}{y_2 = -1}$
