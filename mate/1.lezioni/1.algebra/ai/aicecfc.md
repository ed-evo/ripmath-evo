Risolviamo l'equazione
$$
\textcolor{red}{z^2 - 7z + 12 = 0}
$$
applichiamo la formula

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}$
$\textcolor{blue}{b = -7}$
$\textcolor{blue}{c = 12}$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{-(-7) \pm \sqrt{(-7)^2 - 4(1)(12)}}{2(1)}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 48}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{1}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm 1}{2}}
$$

Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_1 = \frac{7 - 1}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{z_2 = \frac{7 + 1}{2} = \frac{8}{2} = 4}
$$

Ho quindi le soluzioni
$\textcolor{red}{z_1 = 3} \quad \textcolor{red}{z_2 = 4}$
