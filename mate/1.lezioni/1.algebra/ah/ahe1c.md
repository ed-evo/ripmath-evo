Risolviamo l'equazione
$$
\textcolor{red}{t^2 - 5t + 6 = 0}
$$
applichiamo la formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$
Abbiamo:
$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 1}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = -5}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = 6}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{-(-5) \pm \sqrt{(-5)^2 - 4(1)(6)}}{2(1)}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 24}}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{1}}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm 1}{2}}
$$
Ora prendo una volta il meno ed una volta il più
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_1 = \frac{5 - 1}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{t_2 = \frac{5 + 1}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3}
$$
Ho quindi le soluzioni
$$
\textcolor{red}{t_1 = 2 \quad t_2 = 3}
$$
