Risolvo la seconda equazione
$$
\textcolor{blue}{3x^2 - 7x + 2 = 0}
$$

Applico la formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}}
$$

Abbiamo:
$$
\textcolor{blue}{a = 3}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{b = -7}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{c = 2}
$$

Sostituiamo nella formula
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{-(-7) \pm \sqrt{(-7)^2 - 4(3)(2)}}{2 \cdot 3}}
$$

Eseguo i calcoli
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_{1,2} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{49 - 24}}{6}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{25}}{6}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{= \frac{7 \pm 5}{6}}
$$

Adesso devo prendere una volta il più ed una volta il meno
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = \frac{7 + 5}{6} = 2}
$$
$$
\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = \frac{7 - 5}{6} = \frac{1}{3}}
$$
