# Calcoli

$$\textcolor{blue}{x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0}$$

È un'equazione biquadratica: pongo

$$\textcolor{blue}{x^2 = y \quad x^4 = y^2}$$

quindi devo risolvere

$$\textcolor{blue}{y^2 - 5y + 4 = 0}$$

Uso la formula risolutiva:

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{5^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot 4}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 - 16}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{9}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{blue}{y_{1,2} = \frac{5 \pm 3}{2}}
$$

$$\textcolor{blue}{y_1 = 4}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{y_2 = 1}$$

Ora devo trovare la $$\textcolor{blue}{x}$$, quindi devo risolvere le due equazioni

$$\textcolor{blue}{x^2 = 4 \quad x^2 = 1}$$

la prima mi dà le soluzioni

$$\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = +2}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = -2}$$

la seconda mi dà le soluzioni

$$\textcolor{blue}{x_1 = +1}$$
$$\textcolor{blue}{x_2 = -1}$$
