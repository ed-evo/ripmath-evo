# Tabella di riepilogo

## [Rettangolo]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = a \cdot b}$$
$$\textcolor{red}{A_s = \overline{BC} \cdot \overline{AB}}$$

## [Quadrato]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = a^2}$$
$$\textcolor{red}{A_s = \overline{BC}^2}$$

## [Parallelogramma]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = a \cdot h}$$
$$\textcolor{red}{A_s = \overline{BC} \cdot \overline{AH}}$$

## [Triangolo]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABC) = \frac{a \cdot h}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABC) = \frac{\overline{BC} \cdot \overline{AH}}{2}}
$$

## [Rombo]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = \frac{d_1 \cdot d_2}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = \frac{\overline{BD} \cdot \overline{AC}}{2}}
$$

## [Trapezio]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = \frac{(b_1 + b_2) \cdot h}{2}}
$$
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCD) = \frac{(\overline{BC} + \overline{AD}) \cdot \overline{AH}}{2}}
$$

## [Poligono circoscritto]{.text-blue-darken-4}
$$
\textcolor{red}{A_s(ABCDE) = \frac{2p \cdot r}{2} = p \cdot r}
$$
