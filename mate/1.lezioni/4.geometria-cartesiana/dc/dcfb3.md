Equazione della retta parallela alla retta $$AB$$ ($$y = \frac{3}{4}x + 4$$) passante per il punto $$C(-1, -3)$$

[$$
y - y_1 = m_1(x - x_1)
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
x_1 = -1
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
y_1 = -3
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
m_1 = \frac{3}{4}
$$]{.text-blue}

[$$
y - (-3) = \frac{3}{4}[x - (-1)]
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
y + 3 = \frac{3}{4}(x + 1)
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
y + 3 = \frac{3}{4}x + \frac{3}{4}
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
y = \frac{3}{4}x + \frac{3}{4} - 3
$$]{.text-blue}
[$$
y = \frac{3}{4}x - \frac{9}{4}
$$]{.text-blue}
