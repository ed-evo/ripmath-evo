$$\textcolor{red}{x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0}$$

applico la [formula risolutiva](../../a/af/afcc.html)

$$
\textcolor{red}{x_{1,2} = \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{9 - 4(1)(2)}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{= \frac{3 \pm \sqrt{1}}{2}}
$$

$$
\textcolor{red}{= \frac{3 \pm 1}{2}}
$$

quindi

$$\textcolor{red}{x_1 = 1 \quad x_2 = 2}$$
